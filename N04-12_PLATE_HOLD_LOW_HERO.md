# N04-12 — PLATE-HOLD / LOW HERO

**Status:** DRAFT FOR VISUAL REVIEW  
**Profile:** Human-scale low-angle presentation of one selected plated product.  
**Reference use:** Low viewpoint, hand-supported plate pose, selective food focus, warm directional contrast, and intimate photographic character only.

## CAMERA

CAMERA / POV — PLATE-HOLD LOW HERO. Full-frame sensor; optical axis approximately 15° above the level plate plane; level roll. 160 mm full-frame lens; f/2.4; ISO 400 baseline. Use a 9:16 portrait frame. Show the complete selected food and the N03 vessel. Keep the food, plate edge, and supporting hand within the frame; do not cut through the product or the food-to-plate boundary.

Authorize exactly one natural human hand supporting the plate at its outer edge, with physically plausible contact and weight. The hand is part of this profile only. No face, body, sleeve, clothing, second hand, or additional prop. Keep the plate level and the food subject to gravity; do not tilt or reshape the serving to face the camera.

FOCUS — Place one narrow, coherent focus island on the hero food surface, not on the hand or plate rim. Food texture in this region is critically sharp. Let the near and rear food planes soften progressively and naturally while keeping the overall dish identifiable. The supporting hand may be slightly softer than the food but must remain anatomically plausible. No fake bokeh, blur mask, focus stack, or all-over sharpening.

## VISUAL LOOK — INTIMATE WARM PRESENTATION

Use a warm directional key from high camera-left, with restrained fill. Shape the food with selective warm ivory highlights and rich, appetizing midtones. Keep the brightest reflections localized and textured. Create soft-edged, dimensional warm shadows with visible detail; no featureless black shadow masses and no global orange cast.

Retain the reference's intimate, human-scale food presentation without transferring its textile or dark room. Keep color faithful to the selected recipe, texture natural, and food geometry physically credible. The hand must read as a simple supporting gesture, not a lifestyle scene. Avoid over-sharpening, HDR, plastic gloss, artificial saturation, and theatrical lighting effects.

## OUTPUT AND FIDELITY

- Deliver a true RGBA PNG. The food, N03 vessel, authorized supporting hand, and physically attached soft shadows are foreground; everything else has alpha zero.
- No sweater, clothing, room, tabletop, background, typography, watermark, social UI, or additional object.
- No steam, vapor, smoke, mist, fog, or atmospheric plume.
- N02 controls the food and recipe; N03 controls the plate. This profile alone authorizes one supporting hand and does not authorize other new scene content.
