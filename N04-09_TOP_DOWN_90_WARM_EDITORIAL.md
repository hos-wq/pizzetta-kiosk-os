# N04-09 — TOP-DOWN 90° / WARM EDITORIAL

**Status:** DRAFT FOR VISUAL REVIEW  
**Profile:** Full-subject overhead product photograph with warm editorial light and natural food texture.  
**Reference use:** Angle, spacing, exposure, and photographic character only; no visual scene objects are imported.

## CAMERA

CAMERA / POV — TOP-DOWN 90°. Full-frame sensor; optical axis exactly perpendicular to the level subject support plane at 90°; zero roll. 100 mm full-frame lens; f/5.6; ISO 400 baseline. Use a 9:16 portrait frame. Center the selected whole product with balanced clear space above and below; retain its complete silhouette and all serviceware required by N03. Keep circles and vessel rims physically circular in the overhead view. No perspective tilt, camera roll, or oblique three-quarter appearance.

FOCUS — Use moderate, natural optical depth of field sufficient to keep the complete food surface and its recipe-defining components clearly legible. Small height differences may soften gently, but do not create Hero-style foreground/rear blur or artificial all-over sharpening. No focus stacking, painted blur, or fabricated bokeh.

## VISUAL LOOK — WARM EDITORIAL TOP-DOWN

Create a grounded, high-quality editorial food photograph with the clear, direct overhead arrangement of the reference. A warm directional key from upper camera-left gives the food dimensional relief and casts soft, physically plausible shadows. Restrained fill preserves readable detail in darker food regions without flattening the light direction.

Use warm ivory highlights on naturally pale or reflective food surfaces, with localized bright reflections and smooth roll-off. Keep cheese, sauce, crust, and other ingredients textured around the brightest areas. Use rich, ingredient-faithful color separation and moderate photographic contrast: warm golden crust and speculars where the selected N02 permits them; preserve the canonical reds, greens, creams, and browns without a global amber wash.

Keep the image tactile and photographic, not a flat catalog cutout and not a stylized illustration. Preserve natural irregularities and small-scale texture that are already part of the selected food. Avoid aggressive global S-curves, HDR, crushed shadows, halos, plastic gloss, procedural repetition, and excessive sharpening. Photographic shadows remain warm and detailed, never featureless black.

## OUTPUT AND FIDELITY

- Deliver a true RGBA PNG. Pixels outside the selected food, its N03 vessel, and physically attached soft contact/cast shadows have alpha zero.
- No table, wood grain, napkin, extra plate, garnish, text, watermark, social UI, or decorative background.
- No steam, vapor, smoke, mist, fog, or atmospheric plume.
- N02 controls all food identity and recipe facts; N03 controls vessel identity. This profile changes photography only.
