**Status:** DRAFT FOR VISUAL REVIEW  
**Profile:** Close-focus food detail with high chromatic depth, appetizing microtexture, and warm selective reflections.

## CAMERA

CAMERA / POV — MACRO DETAIL. Full-frame sensor; close-focus 100 mm macro lens; f/5.6; ISO 400 baseline. Aim the optical axis perpendicular to the selected detail plane where practical; zero roll. Use a 9:16 portrait frame with intentional detail cropping permitted. The selected N02 detail fills the frame; do not imply that the crop contains an unverified extra ingredient or a different food.

FOCUS — Keep a substantial contiguous food-detail region critically resolved, with natural optical falloff across height and distance. Preserve readable material relief within the focal region; allow outlying edges or taller elements to soften gently. Do not force shallow Hero bokeh over the entire macro plane. No focus stacking, invented blur, digital clarity, or indiscriminate edge sharpening.

## VISUAL LOOK — CHROMATIC MACRO

Use a directional warm key from upper camera-left to reveal real surface relief, with controlled fill that keeps detail visible inside folds and overlaps. Produce high local contrast and deep, layered color without crushing texture. Warm selective reflections may brighten naturally pale, moist, or glossy surfaces to warm ivory or golden-white; keep highlights localized and preserve surrounding material detail.

Render authentic color depth: maintain the ingredient-specific hues and tonal differences established by N02. Warmth belongs in the illumination and reflections, not as a uniform orange filter. Deep crevices retain color and texture rather than becoming black. The food should feel tactile and appetizing, with genuine irregularity and physically plausible moisture only where the canonical recipe supports it.

Do not manufacture salt flakes, droplets, oil, dressing, herbs, flowers, seeds, char marks, pores, or repeated microtexture. Avoid HDR halos, plastic gloss, surreal sharpness, noise masquerading as texture, and oversaturated color. No broad clipped white patches.
