Pizzetta Kiosk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Pizzetta Kiosk uses account information from Google or Meta only to identify the customer, remember orders, operate loyalty points, and provide order support. We store the provider subject, display name, email when available, order history, loyalty ledger, and messages linked to an order.

We do not sell customer data. Payment screenshots are used only to review a GCash order and are retained only for the operational period required by Pizzetta. Staff access is limited by role and branch.

To request access, correction, or deletion, use the data deletion page or contact otherbadhabits.makati@gmail.com.

Cookies and sessions

Pizzetta Kiosk uses secure session cookies for signed-in customer and staff sessions. Guests may place an order without creating an account.
