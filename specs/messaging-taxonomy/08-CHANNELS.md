# CHANNEL INTEGRATION POLICY

A Person may have multiple Channel Identities.

Initial relevant channel concepts:

MESSENGER
INSTAGRAM
WHATSAPP
FACEBOOK
THREADS
GOOGLE / GOOGLE BUSINESS only if an actual supported integration exists
OTHER

Do not pretend a provider integration exists when it does not.

==================================================
CHANNEL ICONS

Do NOT use Lucide replacements for platform identities.

Use official platform brand assets where permitted.

Sources must be official/current brand-resource packages.

Store approved application assets in the BR Cloudinary account or appropriate BR static asset storage.

Do not redraw platform logos.

Do not modify official marks contrary to their brand requirements.

BR OS ordinary interface/action icons continue using Lucide.

==================================================
OPEN EXTERNAL CONVERSATION

Person dossier and Mobile Person card should provide recognizable channel icons.

Click/tap should open the most specific supported external conversation destination.

Provider-specific implementation MUST be verified.

Do not invent undocumented deep links.

If exact conversation deep linking is unavailable, use the best safe supported destination and communicate the limitation through UI behavior.

==================================================
WHATSAPP

Use supported WhatsApp click-to-chat/deep-link mechanisms when applicable.

==================================================
MESSENGER

Use supported Meta Messenger links/mechanisms when applicable.

==================================================
INSTAGRAM / THREADS / OTHER

Verify actual supported current mechanism before implementation.

Do not assume Messenger behavior applies to other Meta products.

==================================================
CHANNEL IDENTITY

Channel identity belongs to Person resolution.

One Person may have:

Messenger identity
+
WhatsApp identity
+
Instagram identity

without becoming three People.

Merging/linking ambiguous identities requires safe resolution/Alan confirmation.
